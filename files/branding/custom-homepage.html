Welcome to UCLA Dataverse

A research data repository for UCLA researchers to share, archive, and cite research data. Managed by the UCLA Library Data Science Center.

Get Started

Sharing your data is easy:

  1. Create an account or log in with your UCLA credentials
  2. Request a dataverse for your project, lab, or department
  3. Upload your files and add metadata descriptions
  4. Publish to receive a DOI for citation
